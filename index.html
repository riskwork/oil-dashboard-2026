    Terms, sources and method

    Brent / WTI: the world oil price and the American oil price. Codes BZ=F and CL=F on Yahoo Finance.
    Futures price: the agreed price today for oil delivered next month. This is the price quoted in the news. The physical cargo price ("Dated Brent") can be higher in a crisis; it reached a record of about $145 in April.
    Per barrel (bbl): one barrel is 159 litres.
    Moving average (20-day, 50-day): the average price over the last 20 or 50 trading days. Price above both means the recent trend is up.
    Volatility: standard deviation of daily price changes over 21 trading days, multiplied by √252 to give a yearly figure.
    Drawdown: the percentage by which the price is below its highest close so far this year.
    US shale cost floor (about $62): a rough cost of drilling a new US shale well, from industry surveys before 2026. Below it, US drilling slows and supply shrinks over months. It is a reference line, not a forecast.
    Data: daily closing prices from Yahoo Finance; 194 trading days from 2 January to 9 October 2026, US exchange holidays excluded. Prices were checked against Reuters, Bloomberg and TASS reports. Events and forecasts link to their sources.
