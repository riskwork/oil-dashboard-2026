# Oil Price Dashboard 2026

Interactive dashboard of Brent and WTI crude oil prices from 2 January to 9 October 2026: daily prices, the Brent–WTI gap, volatility and drawdown, key market events, published forecasts, and what it means for Syria.

**View it:** https://riskwork.github.io/oil-dashboard-2026/

Data: Yahoo Finance front-month futures closes (Brent BZ=F, WTI CL=F). Events and forecasts link to their sources.

Analysis by Alaa Sardar. Views are my own and do not represent any institution. Not investment advice.
